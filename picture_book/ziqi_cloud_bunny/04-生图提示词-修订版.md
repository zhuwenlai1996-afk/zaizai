# 04 · 生图提示词 · 修订版

> 这一版相比原案有 4 个核心改动：
> 1. **删除**"请参考 image_0.png"这种 AI 读不懂的中文指令，改成在工具的"参考图通道"上传
> 2. **删除**"在画面中加入中文文字"——文字后期排版贴上，不让 AI 生成中文（会乱码）
> 3. **加入构图节奏**：每页指定具体镜头（仰视/俯视/特写/全景），避免每页都"主体居中"
> 4. **应用集星主线伏笔**：P4/P6/P8 各加一颗小星星飞起的细节，与 P9 的星星汇聚呼应

---

## 通用模板（每页都套）

每页 prompt = `[A 角色锚点]` + `[B 本页场景]` + `[C 构图镜头]` + `[D 风格]` + `[E 排版预留]`

```
[A 角色锚点]
A 5.5-year-old Chinese girl named Ziqi, illustrated in storybook style:
black hair in two low pigtails, delicate pearl hair clip on her hairline,
round cute face, big round eyes, bright confident smile,
wearing a multilayered white tulle princess dress with a soft sky-blue ribbon at the waist,
white sandals. Consistent appearance across all pages.

Companion: a fluffy round cloud-bunny named Mianhua,
white as a cloud, gentle eyes, small pale-blue ribbon around its neck.

[B 本页场景]
（每页不同，见下文）

[C 构图镜头]
（每页不同，见下文）

[D 风格]
warm watercolor children's book illustration, soft natural lighting,
low-saturation but bright colors, dreamy yet grounded,
clean composition, gentle background, cozy and safe atmosphere,
suitable for printing in a physical picture book, high resolution.

[E 排版预留]
Leave the bottom 25% of the canvas as clean, uncluttered space for text overlay.
DO NOT generate any text, letters, or Chinese characters in the image.
```

**统一负面提示词（英文，主流图模通用）：**
```
text, letters, chinese characters, watermark, signature, logo,
twins, multiple children, duplicate face, distorted face, deformed eyes,
extra fingers, missing fingers, malformed hands, bad anatomy,
adult-looking, scary, dark, gloomy, plastic skin, hyper-realistic,
cluttered background, low resolution, jpeg artifacts, AI artifacts
```

**中文版负面提示词（即梦/豆包等中文模型用）：**
```
不要文字、不要字母、不要中文字、不要水印、不要签名、不要 logo、
不要双胞胎、不要多个孩子、不要重复人脸、不要变形脸、不要变形眼睛、
不要多余手指、不要缺失手指、不要畸形手、不要错误结构、
不要成人化、不要恐怖、不要阴暗、不要塑料感、不要过度写实、
不要杂乱背景、不要低清晰度、不要 AI 伪影
```

## 封面

**[C 构图镜头]** Medium close-up, frontal view, character occupies the upper-center portion of the frame. Title space reserved at the very top, text-overlay space at the bottom 20%.

## P1 · 起 · 礼物

**画面文字：** 六一节的早晨，爸爸妈妈送给子琪一只毛茸茸的云朵兔子，名字叫小棉花。

**[B 场景]**
> Early morning bedroom scene. Ziqi sits on her bed beside a sunlit window, holding the fluffy cloud-bunny Mianhua in her arms with a delighted, surprised expression. A gift box with a pale-blue ribbon lies open beside her. Soft morning light streams through gauzy curtains, dust motes glowing in the sunbeams. A small framed photo of her parents sits on the bedside table (subtle background detail establishing the parents).

**[C 构图镜头]** Medium shot, three-quarter angle from slightly above. Ziqi positioned in the right-third of the frame following rule of thirds. Window light from the left.

## P2 · 起 · 飞翔

**画面文字：** 小棉花眨眨眼睛，拉着子琪的手，一起飞出了窗外，飞进温柔的云朵里。

**[B 场景]**
> Ziqi and Mianhua flying together through a vast sky filled with soft fluffy clouds. Mianhua gently holds Ziqi's hand. Below them, a tiny city with twinkling rooftops. Pastel pink and blue sky, a few birds in the distance. Ziqi's dress and pigtails flutter gently in the wind. Her face glows with wonder.

**[C 构图镜头]** **Wide aerial establishing shot**, slightly bird's-eye view. Characters small in the upper-third, sky and clouds dominate. Strong contrast with P1's enclosed indoor scene to create page-turn surprise.

## P3 · 承 · 害怕但前进

**画面文字：** 迷雾森林好黑呀，子琪有点害怕，但她紧紧牵着小棉花，一步一步往前走。

**[B 场景]**
> Ziqi and Mianhua walking through a misty, slightly dim forest path. Tall ethereal trees with luminous leaves rise overhead. Soft fog drifts at ground level. Ziqi grips Mianhua's paw tightly, her expression a brave little frown — scared but determined. Mianhua looks up at her reassuringly. A faint glow ahead suggests hope.

**[C 构图镜头]** **Low-angle shot looking up**, emphasizing the tall trees and the smallness of the children — captures a child's perspective of a "big scary forest." Characters in the lower-center, looming canopy fills the upper frame.

## P4 · 承 · 想象力创造

**画面文字：** 子琪发现一束星光，她跟着星光画呀画，画出一座彩虹桥，跑过了森林。

**[B 场景]**
> Ziqi at the forest's edge, joyfully tracing a beam of starlight with her fingertip in the air. Sparkling rainbow lines follow her fingertip and curve forward into a bridge of light spanning a small ravine. Mianhua hops alongside her, cheering. **A single small bright star quietly rises from Ziqi's chest and drifts upward into the sky** — first plant of the "collected stars" thread.

**[C 构图镜头]** **Eye-level shot with strong perspective lines** — the rainbow bridge recedes into the distance, drawing the eye toward hope. Ziqi positioned in the left-third, bridge extending to the right.

## P9 · 合 · 集星揭晓

**画面文字：** 山顶上，三颗小星星汇成一颗大大的勇气星，亮晶晶地落进子琪的心里。

**[B 场景]**
> Ziqi and Mianhua stand on a starry mountaintop under a vast night sky. The three small stars from P4/P6/P8 fly back and merge into one big radiant "Courage Star" hovering in front of Ziqi. The big star then descends and softly enters her chest, leaving a warm golden glow. Mianhua watches with awe. **This is the visual payoff of the entire story arc.**

**[C 构图镜头]** **Low-angle wide shot**, characters silhouetted against an immense star-filled sky. Sense of cosmic awe and accomplishment. Reserve the lower-center for the warm glow on Ziqi's chest.

## 生成顺序建议

1. **先做角色三视图（model sheet）**：让 AI 用上面的 [A 角色锚点] 生成一张"子琪 + 小棉花"的正面/侧面/背面三视图，定稿后所有页面都参考这张。
2. **第二步生成封面**：定型整体画风。
3. **按 P1 → P10 顺序生成**：每页都以"封面 + 三视图"为参考，确保角色一致。
4. **最后生成祝福页**：纯装饰背景最简单，放最后。
5. **集中校色 + CMYK 转换**：印前用 Photoshop 把所有图统一转 CMYK 并稍微提一点饱和度补偿（CMYK 印出来会比 RGB 暗一档）。
6. **InDesign 排版上文字**：字体推荐"站酷快乐体""鸿雷板书""字魂童心体"。
